### Project Description
A cat shapped desktop robo-pet that will judge you and get angry at you when you sit down to study and do not study. 

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
Ever sit down to study, all motivated and ready to make a academic comeback and then get burnt out in 5 minutes?
Well now, you can do the same and have a non sentient cat be pissed off at you.

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)
It cant do much, but the judgy cat can judge you and make you feel bad for not studying
unless you just ignore it.
